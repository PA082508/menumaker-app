# Director's guide — electronic enrollment forms

**Internal · plain-language · owner: Nikolay · 2026-07-25**

A short, practical guide to the electronic enrollment forms for directors. No jargon — just
what you'll see and tap.

> **Reminder (production status):** while we await the Office of Nutrition's approval,
> **paper stays the official record** for CACFP enrollment and income-eligibility. The
> electronic forms run *alongside* paper, not instead of it. See
> [`esignature-system-description.md`](./esignature-system-description.md) → "Production use status."

## 1. Signing a form: three ways, one habit

When you reach a signature slot on a form, you have three options — pick whichever is
comfortable, the result is the same legal signature:

- **Draw** — sign with your finger or a stylus in the box. It scales to fit the slot.
- **Type** — type your name and choose a handwriting style (a few script faces to pick from).
- **Apply a saved signature** — one tap drops in the signature you saved before.

**Your first signature becomes your sample.** The first time you draw or type, you can
choose **"Remember"** — from then on, that same signature is one tap away on every later
form. You never have to redraw it. (Saved signatures are **private to you** — no one else in
the organization can see or use yours.)

## 2. The date signs itself

You don't fill in the date next to a signature. **It stamps automatically** the moment you
sign — today's date, read-only. If you clear the signature, the date clears with it. You can
never back-date or pre-fill it. (Ordinary data dates — birthday, first day, effective dates —
are normal fields you still type.)

## 3. "View original form" — see the real thing before you approve

In the enrollment Inbox, when you open a submission, tap **"View original form."** Instead of
a table of fields, you see the **actual official state form** filled in with the family's
answers **and the parent's signature in place** — exactly as it will look on paper. Your own
countersignature lands in the real "Program Administrator / Designee" slot right there, before
you Approve.

## 4. Printing

From the same view, **Print** produces the real **2-page government form** — the state layout,
not a screenshot. Print on demand whenever you need a paper copy for the file.

## 5. Annual review rows (the "Parent/Guardian Initials / Date of Review" lines)

On a **first enrollment**, those review rows are **locked** and show a small note —
"completed at yearly renewal." That's on purpose: a parent can't initial next year's reviews
early. On a **renewal**, the rows **open one at a time**, in order — so each year's review is
its own dated line.

## 6. Renewals

At renewal, the family gets a renewal link. The next review row opens, the parent initials and
it stamps the date, and you countersign the same way. Nothing from the original enrollment is
rewritten — a renewal adds to the record, it doesn't overwrite it.

## Quick answers

| You want to… | Do this |
|--------------|---------|
| Sign fast every time | Draw/type once, tap **Remember** → one-tap after |
| Fix the date | You can't (and shouldn't) — it stamps itself when you sign |
| See the real form, not fields | **View original form** in the Inbox |
| Get a paper copy | **Print** — comes out as the official 2-page form |
| Why are review rows greyed out? | First enrollment locks them; they open at renewal, one per year |
| Someone else's saved signature | You can't see it — saved signatures are private to each person |
