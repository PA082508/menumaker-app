> **Rule #1 of this center: the safety of the child. Everything below exists for that rule
> alone.** The seat count you type in, the second tap the phone asks for, the run that refuses to
> close — none of it is paperwork. It is the rule, made practical.

**One page. Your phone, one hand, at the kerb.**

---

## 1. Your phone is registered once

The director opens a registration link on **your** phone, once. After that the phone knows the
center, and **your PIN** says it is you — on every single tap. No app store, no password.
*[ships — move 2-T]*

Open **/safepass/driver**, type your 4-digit staff PIN, and you are in.

## 2. Start the run

Pick **Morning — to school**, **Afternoon — from school** or **Field trip**, type the bus and
**how many seats it has**. *[ships — move 2-T]*

The seat count belongs to **this bus on this run** — it is a limit, not a suggestion, and the
whole run is measured against it afterwards.

## 3. The route reads like the road

Children are grouped **by school**, in the order you drive. Each name has one big button. The
counter at the top always shows **how many are on board out of how many seats**.
*[ships — move 2-T]*

- **On bus** — tap when the child is aboard, at the school.
- **Off** — tap when the child steps off, at the school.

That tap is signed with **your name**. It is the record.

## 4. When the bus is full

The phone will **refuse** to board one more child and offer a second run instead. If a child must
travel anyway — a real emergency at the kerb — tap that child a **second time**: the button
changes to **Board anyway**, and the trip is recorded as **over capacity** for the director to
see. *[ships — move 2-T]*

Never quietly. Either it fits, or it is written down.

## 5. The run will not close with a child on the bus

**Run completed** is refused while anyone is still marked on board — and the phone **names them**,
so you know exactly who to go and look for. Walk the aisle, tap them off, then close.
*[ships — move 2-T]*

## 6. No signal, or a paper day

Fill the **paper checklist** from the glovebox as before. When you are back in range, open the
run and **photograph the sheet** — it attaches to that run. The paper stays with the office.
*[ships — move 2-T]*

---

### Field trips are the same trip

Same screen, same taps, same full-bus rule. One difference: on an outing the children **stay in
the center's care all day**, so nothing you tap goes into their attendance as a departure or a
return. Your taps answer one question only: **who is on the bus right now.**

### If something looks wrong

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| "This phone is not registered" | The link was never opened here, or access was revoked — call the director. |
| "That PIN is not recognised" | Wrong PIN, or your record is inactive — call the office. |
| "The bus is full" | Exactly that. Second run, or a deliberate second tap. |
| "Still on the bus: …" | Those children are still marked aboard. Go and check the seats. |
| Nothing saves | Use the paper sheet and photograph it later. Never guess at the end of the day. |
