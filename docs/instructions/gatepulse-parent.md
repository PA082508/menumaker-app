> **Rule #1 of this center: the safety of your child. Everything below exists for that rule
> alone.** The extra half-second of waiting for a green check, the photo on file, the teacher
> signing by name — all of it is that one rule, made practical.

**One minute at the front desk — your phone number and a photo — then, from that day until
your child's last day with us, it's two taps a day: Drop Off in the morning, Pick Up in the
evening, and the green ✅ that says your child is safe. No codes, no passwords, no cost.**

**One page. You need your phone and nothing else.**

---

## 1. You register once — at the front desk

Come to the desk with your phone. **Staff taps Register next to your name and takes your photo —
that's how teachers recognize you at the door.** That is the whole registration: no app store, no
password, no code to remember. *[built today]*

Your **phone number is your key.** Only the number the center registered can sign in.

## 2. Sign in — your number, once

Open the center's SafePass link (posted at the door, or ask staff to send it). Type **your own
phone number**. That's it — you're in. *[built today]*

Then tap your browser's **Share → Add to Home Screen**. GatePulse becomes an icon on your phone
and opens straight to your child. Your phone stays signed in for 30 days and renews itself as
you use it. *[built today]*

## 3. Drop off / pick up — two taps

- Tap your child, then **Drop Off** (morning) or **Pick Up** (afternoon).
- The teacher's tablet lights up in your child's room.
- **Wait for the green ✅** — it names the teacher who accepted or released your child, with the
  time. Until you see it, nothing has been recorded: don't leave on a request alone. *[built today]*

That green check is your receipt. The same two records appear under **Today** on your screen.

## 3a. What you'll hear

The tablet in your child's room **says the record out loud** the moment it is written.
It is the spoken half of the same receipt you see on your phone — the room hears it,
so a mistake is caught while you are still standing there, not a week later.

- When you tap **I'm here**, before any teacher has touched anything, the room hears
  **“Maria Lopez is here for Emma Carter.”** That is an announcement, not a record —
  nothing has been signed yet, and your child has not been released to anyone.
- When the teacher accepts your child in the morning:
  **“Emma Carter — accepted, 8:02.”**
- When the teacher releases your child to you or to someone you authorized:
  **“Emma Carter — released to Maria Lopez, 5:12.”**

Three things are said aloud every time — **who, to whom, and at what minute** — and
they are exactly the three things written into the record and shown on your phone.

**If what you hear is not what is happening — say so immediately.** A wrong name or a
wrong person spoken aloud means a wrong record was just written, and the fastest fix
is the one made at the door.

The room may be in quiet hour (nap time), and then you will hear nothing at all. That
changes nothing about the record: your green ✅ on your own phone is the receipt, and
it always appears.

## 4. Who else may pick up your child

Everyone you authorize gets their **own** phone number registered — and **each of them is
photographed at their own registration**, so the teacher at the door knows the face as well as the
name. A key is personal and is never shared or passed around. The center's enrollment form takes up to **five** authorized
people; add or remove them at the office at any time. *[built today]*

**One number, all your children.** If you have two or three children in the center, your single
number covers all of them — you pick the child on screen. *[built today]*

## 5. No smartphone? Nothing breaks

Ask staff for a **one-time code**. They read you a 6-digit number, you type it on the sign-in
screen, and you're in for that visit. The code lasts 15 minutes and works once. *[built today]*

### If something looks wrong

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| "This number is not registered yet" | The desk hasn't tapped Register for your number — ask staff. |
| "You're no longer authorized for this child" | The center changed the pickup list. The office can explain and fix it. |
| "Could not load…" | Connection only — nothing was lost; try again in a moment. |
| No green ✅ after a minute | The room hasn't accepted yet. Go to the desk rather than leaving. |

**Your phone is your key — treat it like the key to the building. Lost or changed phone: tell
the office and they re-register you in one tap.**
