# A paper income form arrived at your centre

**If a paper income form (IEA) or an income-based waiver arrives at your center — hand it
to the Play Academy Director (Tatiana) immediately.**

**Do not enter or scan it yourself.** Income forms are entered by household on
**Paper applications**, and that screen is not part of a centre director's work.

## Why it is not yours

An income application says what a family earns. That is the one thing a centre does not
need in order to feed a child, and the system is built so you never see it: the forms do
not appear in your inbox, they are not counted in your red number, and the determination
they carry is made at the organisation level.

What you do see, on the child's card, is a content-free marker that a determination is on
file. That is all a centre needs.

## What to do, in order

1. Do not photograph it, do not type it in, do not file it in the child's card.
2. Hand the paper to Tatiana — in person, or by internal mail, the same day.
3. If a parent hands it to you at the door, take it and tell them it goes to the office.

## What happens next

Tatiana enters it by household on **Paper applications**: one entry writes the record for
every child in that family, with the date printed on the paper, and the paper is marked as
filed. The child's card then shows the determination on file — and your yellow banner
("N Free/Reduced children without a current IEA on file") goes down by that family.

## The food enrollment form is different

The **CACFP enrollment form** — the one about days and meals — **is** your work: it carries
no income and needs no countersignature. It stays in your inbox, and you approve it.
