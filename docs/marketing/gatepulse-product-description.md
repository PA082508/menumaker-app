# GatePulse — product description

**Built on the NurturePulse platform · dual-purpose document · owner: Nikolay · 2026-07-26**

> **Rule #1: the safety of the child. Everything in this product exists for that rule alone.**
> It is the deciding question for every feature: does it serve rule #1? Speed, elegance and
> fewer taps are legitimate arguments until they start costing the truth of who took a child
> and who handed them over — past that line they lose, every time.
>
> **How to read this.** GatePulse serves two audiences with **two separate framings**. The
> **Regulatory framing** describes the attendance and release system as presented to a licensing
> agency — **no AI is part of the regulatory record path.** The **Product / B2B framing**
> describes the commercial offering. Keep the framings distinct; do not import product language
> into regulatory materials. Companion document: [`enrollpulse-product-description.md`].
> Capability status is labeled **[live]**, **[ships with teacher release]** or **Planned** —
> nothing aspirational is written as if it existed.

## One line

**Your phone is your key.** GatePulse records every child's arrival and release as a two-sided,
time-stamped, personally-signed event — a registered adult on one side, a named teacher on the
other — without an app store, a password, a card or a fob.

What that means to a parent, in their own words:

**One minute at the front desk — your phone number and a photo — then, from that day until
your child's last day with us, it's two taps a day: Drop Off in the morning, Pick Up in the
evening, and the green ✅ that says your child is safe. No codes, no passwords, no cost.**

## A. Regulatory framing — attendance and release, no AI in the record path

What a licensor sees:

- **Every release is signed by a person, not a device.** The teacher who actually received or
  released the child confirms under their **own name** — a check-in-scoped identity, recorded
  with the minute. A tablet, a room or a shift never signs on a person's behalf. *[live: PIN
  identity; ships with teacher release: named tiles]*
- **Only authorized adults.** The right to collect a child comes from the enrollment record
  approved by the center — GatePulse turns on **electronic access** for people already on that
  list, and never invents one. Revoking app access and removing a person from the pickup list
  are two deliberately separate levers. *[live]*
- **A personal key.** Access is bound to the adult's **own registered phone number**; keys are
  not shared, passed around or printed. Devices are trusted per phone and can be revoked
  centrally at any time. *[live]*
- **Nothing is recorded that did not happen.** A parent's request is not a record: until a
  teacher confirms, the event is open and visibly unconfirmed on both screens. *[live]*
- **Exception paths are labeled, not hidden.** Where a handoff is confirmed outside the normal
  path — no teacher checked in — the record carries an explicit flag and stays in the director's
  log. Exceptions remain visible instead of blending into normal traffic. *[ships with teacher
  release]*
- **Paper is never removed.** The paper sign-in panel remains available and legal at all times.
  During the pilot the electronic record runs **parallel to paper**, and **paper remains the official
  record until the center's decision**. *[live]*
- **Photographs: human identification only.** Every authorized adult is photographed at
  registration, with consent given twice over — the parent is told on the release form that all
  authorized adults will be photographed, and the adult themselves ticks the consent before the
  shot is taken. Photos live in a **private** store (a path, never a URL) and are read only
  through short-lived signed links by staff. **No automated facial recognition of any kind** —
  the comparison is made by a person looking at a face. Photos are deleted on revocation or on
  request, and are **never** used publicly — site, social media, marketing or mailings — without
  a separate, explicit consent. An adult who declines is not denied pickup: the record shows
  **"no photo on file"** and staff use a **Photo ID check** instead. *[ships with teacher release]*
- **Center-local time.** A day is the **center's own local day** on every screen and every
  report, so one event never carries two dates. *[live]*
- **No AI anywhere in the record path.** Attendance and release records are produced by explicit
  human action only.

**Status honesty (regulatory):** capabilities not yet live are labeled **Planned** or
**[ships with teacher release]** and are never described in the present tense to an agency.

## B. Product / B2B framing — the door that runs itself

**The problem.** Two things decide whether a door system works: whether the record of **who took
the child** is true and personal, and whether parents actually use it every day. Most products
lose on the second — an app to install, a password to reset, a fob or a card to carry and lose —
and once people work around a system, the first stops being true as well: what gets recorded is
whatever was convenient, not what happened.

**The move.** GatePulse makes the phone the key and the teacher's own name the signature.

- **Zero onboarding friction.** One tap at the desk registers a parent. No install, no invite
  email, no password reset — the parent types their own number and adds the page to their home
  screen. *[live]*
- **Two taps at the door.** Child → Drop Off / Pick Up; a green confirmation names the teacher
  and the minute. *[live]*
- **Two taps in the room.** Card → your own name tile. The signer is chosen and the handoff
  confirmed in a single gesture — the fastest honest path we could design. *[ships with teacher
  release]*
- **Family-shaped, not record-shaped.** One number covers all of a family's children; each
  authorized adult carries their own key. *[live]*
- **Graceful under failure.** No smartphone → a staff-issued one-time code. No check-in → an
  explicitly flagged PIN path. No network → paper, reconciled after. The door never stops.
  *[live / ships with teacher release]*
- **The director's pocket.** Register, revoke, reassign, and read the log from an iPhone on the
  floor. Alerts are actionable where they appear. *[ships with teacher release]*
- **It tells you when the record has drifted from reality.** A teacher repeatedly working a room
  they aren't assigned to produces a named, one-tap-fixable prompt — because a release record is
  only as true as the assignments behind it. *[ships with teacher release]*

- **It ports to any center without configuring anything.** The half of the day a mark belongs to
  is decided by the **actual time of the event** against the center's own noon, and the record
  never depends on a school calendar or a term schedule: different schools simply produce
  different facts. A center can start on a Monday with no timetable set up, and the sheets come
  out right. *[ships with teacher release]*
- **Transport arm — custody at the school door.** For school-age children the boundary of care is
  the school door itself: the driver taps each child aboard at the school and off at the school,
  and that tap — **signed by the driver, by name** — is what lands in the record. The trip sheet
  prints per run in the same official layout as the classroom sheets. *Planned.*

**Draft B2B theses** *(for review, not yet positioning copy)*

1. *Sell the signature, not the check-in.* Competitors sell attendance; the defensible asset is
   a per-event, per-person signature a licensor accepts.
2. *Zero-install is the wedge.* Parent adoption is where door products die. No app store, no
   password — the number is the key.
3. *Exceptions as a feature.* Flagged fallbacks are what make the normal path trustworthy; a
   system with no exception path gets worked around silently.
4. *Attach, don't stand alone.* GatePulse and EnrollPulse share one roster, one family and one
   center — the door reads the enrollment record instead of a second list.
5. *Reality-drift detection is unowned ground.* Nobody in this category tells the operator that
   their own data has stopped matching the building.
