Play Academy Inc. — CACFP Compliance Platform
Overview for the Department of Education and Workforce, Office of Nutrition

Who we are: Play Academy Inc. operates three licensed child care centers in Northeast Ohio —
Wickliffe, Highland Heights, and Parma Heights — all participating in the Child and Adult Care
Food Program. Over the past months we have developed an internal child care management
platform whose primary purpose is to strengthen our CACFP compliance and record keeping. We
are seeking the Office of Nutrition's review and approval of our electronic forms before any
production use, and we welcome your requirements and consultation.

Electronic Enrollment & Income Eligibility forms: Faithful electronic versions of the current
state forms (CACFP Enrollment, Income Eligibility Application FY2026–27, DCY series),
completed by parents from home via a QR code or link that is pre-scoped to their child's
center. Guided entry reduces errors: date pickers, phone and address validation,
required-field checks, and meal-time selections drawn from the center's actual operating
schedule. Every record can be printed on demand in the state form layout, and all records are
retained for three years plus the current year, consistent with CACFP record-keeping
requirements.

Electronic signature with a verifiable digital trail: Parents sign with a legally binding
electronic signature (drawn or typed). Each signed submission records: a server-authoritative
timestamp, the signature method, the form version, the signer's stated identity, the center,
and the signature image bound to that specific submission's data. Signed records are
cryptographically sealed: a SHA-256 hash binds the form data and signature at the moment of
submission, and sealed records cannot be edited or deleted — any correction creates a new
linked record with a stated reason. The signer's consent to electronic signature, IP address,
and device are recorded with each submission.

Accuracy foundation for meal counts: Meal counts are recorded at point of service against
same-day verified attendance — a meal cannot be recorded for a child who is not checked in.
Child attendance is anchored by a documented hand-off at drop-off and pick-up (our SafePass
process), with timestamps; staff clock in and out through the same system, documenting
supervision hours and ratios.

Administrator oversight and claim preparation: A director review workflow queues for manual
approval only the forms that require an administrator's countersignature; all other completed
forms file automatically with full visibility of what has arrived. Monthly claim data is
assembled from the recorded counts and enrollment / income-eligibility statuses, with rates
applied by the reporting period in effect.

What we are asking: We request the Office's review and approval of our electronic forms, and
your guidance on any requirements for electronic signatures and records. Our target is to
complete this work and have the approved forms in place for the new CACFP program year
beginning October 1, 2026, so we would be grateful to begin the review at your convenience. We
are glad to provide sample form links, printed samples, screenshots, a live demonstration, or
any documentation helpful to your review.
